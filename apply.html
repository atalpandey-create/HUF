Setting Up Your Family HUF

Setting up an HUF is simpler than most people think. It doesn't require complex corporate registrations or registration offices.

Because an HUF is created automatically by birth and marriage, you don't need a government certificate to prove it exists. However, to open bank accounts, buy shares, and file taxes, you must formalise the HUF with a written declaration (Deed) and get a dedicated PAN card.

Follow this 5-step roadmap to establish your HUF bank account, fund it legally, and start trading in the stock market.

STEP 01

Draft the HUF Declaration Deed

The HUF Deed is a declaration on stamp paper stating that the HUF exists, naming the Karta (the head of the family), and listing the names of all the Coparceners (children) and Members (spouse).

Deed Guidelines

  • Stamp Paper: Print the deed on a non-judicial stamp paper of your state (typically ₹100 or ₹200 denomination).
  • Signatures: The Karta must sign the deed, along with two independent witnesses. Some banks also require coparceners to sign.
  • Notarization: It is highly recommended to get the deed notarized by a local notary.

HUF Declaration Deed Template

DECLARATION OF HINDU UNDIVIDED FAMILY (HUF)

I, ______________________ [Name of Karta], son of ______________________ [Father's Name], resident of __________________________________________________ [Address], do hereby solemnly declare as follows:

1. That I am the Karta of ______________________ [Name of Karta] HUF (hereinafter referred to as "the HUF").
2. That the HUF was formed upon my marriage on ______________________ [Date of Marriage].
3. That the HUF consists of the following members:
   
   a) KARTA:
      - Name: ______________________ [Name of Karta], Age: _____, PAN: ________________
   
   b) COPARCENERS (Linear Descendants / Partners by Birth):
      - Name: ______________________ [Name of Son/Daughter 1], Age: _____, Relationship: Son/Daughter
      - Name: ______________________ [Name of Son/Daughter 2], Age: _____, Relationship: Son/Daughter
   
   c) MEMBERS (Spouse / Joined by Marriage):
      - Name: ______________________ [Name of Wife], Age: _____, Relationship: Wife

4. That the HUF is an independent entity for tax purposes and maintains its own bank accounts and investment assets.
5. That all the assets, banking balances, shares, and properties held in the name of the HUF belong to the joint pool of the HUF and are for the benefit of all the members named above.

STEP 02

Apply for the HUF PAN Card

Since the HUF is a separate taxpayer, it must have its own PAN card. You cannot use your personal PAN for HUF investments.

How to Apply:

  1. Online Portal: Visit the official Protean (formerly NSDL) website or UTIITSL portal for PAN applications.
  2. Form 49A: Select Form 49A (for Indian Citizens).
  3. Select Category: Under the "Status of Applicant" dropdown, choose HINDU UNDIVIDED FAMILY.
  4. Naming Convention: The name of the applicant must follow the format: `[Karta's First Name] [Karta's Last Name] HUF` (For example: *RAJESH SHARMA HUF*).
  5. Identify Proofs: Upload the notarized HUF Deed as proof of identity/address. Provide the Karta's individual identity and address proof as well.
  6. Fee Payment: Pay the standard application fee (~₹110 online).

Once submitted, the physical PAN card will be mailed to your address in 10-15 days, and an e-PAN will be sent to your email.

Common PAN Mistake: Never register the HUF name as just a business name (like "Sharma Investments"). The Income Tax department requires it to contain the word HUF (e.g. *Rajesh Sharma HUF*), otherwise the application will be rejected.
STEP 03

Open the HUF Bank Account

All money transacted by the HUF must flow through a dedicated bank account. Most banks allow you to open a Savings or Current account for an HUF.

Documents to carry to the bank:

  • Original HUF PAN Card.
  • Copy of the notarized HUF Deed.
  • Karta's personal ID and Address proof (Aadhaar, Passport, etc.).
  • HUF Rubber Stamp. Get a simple self-inking stamp made from a local vendor. It should read:
    For [HUF NAME]


    Karta

Operational Sops

Signing Authority: The Karta is the sole signing authority for all checks and bank transactions. Other family members cannot sign checks.

Net Banking: You can request net banking and mobile banking under the Karta's registered mobile number.

STEP 04

How to Fund the HUF Legally

This is the most critical step. If you simply transfer your personal salary into the HUF bank account, the tax officer will apply Section 64(2) (Clubbing of Income), meaning all profits earned by the HUF will be taxed in your personal file anyway. To avoid this, use these legal funding routes:

Legal Sourcing Methods

  • Inheritance: Any money or property received through a family will automatically belongs to the HUF and faces zero clubbing issues.
  • Gifts from Non-Members: HUF can receive up to ₹50,000 per year tax-free from friends or distant relatives. Larger gifts are allowed tax-free on special occasions like marriage.
  • Gifts from Members (The Loophole): You can gift money to your HUF. While the *first level of income* (e.g. interest on a fixed deposit) is clubbed with your income, any *reinvested income* (interest earned on that interest, or capital gains from stock trading) is taxed only under the HUF.
Always Use Gift Deeds: For every gift or fund transfer, write a quick "Gift Deed" on a ₹50/100 stamp paper. This proves the money was a gift and explains the tax status to the Income Tax department.

STEP 05

Open Demat and Start Stock Investing

Once your HUF bank account is active, you are ready to open a brokerage account and participate in capital markets.

Linking Your Brokerage

Major discount brokers (like Zerodha, Groww, AngelOne) and full-service brokers (ICICI Direct, HDFC Securities) have dedicated forms for HUF accounts.

  • Demat Account opening: Fill out the HUF demat application. The account will be opened as `[Karta Name] HUF`.
  • KYC Requirements: Provide HUF PAN card, HUF Bank statement, and the Karta's KYC documents.
  • Coparceners' Consent: The broker will ask for the names, PANs, and signatures of all coparceners (children) on a declaration sheet to verify family consent.
  • Nomination: HUF accounts cannot have standard individual nominees because the assets belong to the family. If the Karta passes away, the eldest remaining coparcener automatically becomes the new Karta and takes over the accounts.
Ready to go! Once your broker validates the files (takes 3-5 days), you can transfer funds from your HUF Bank Account to your trading account and start investing in stocks and mutual funds!
